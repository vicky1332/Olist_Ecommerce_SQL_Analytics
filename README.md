# Olist E-Commerce SQL Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end SQL analytics project using the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset** to analyze sales, customers, sellers, products, payments, reviews, revenue, and delivery performance.

The project focuses on answering real-world business questions using **MySQL** and progressively applies SQL techniques ranging from basic aggregations and joins to **CTEs, subqueries, and advanced window functions**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on understanding:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Seller performance
- Product and category performance
- Payment behavior
- Customer satisfaction and reviews
- Delivery and logistics performance
- Revenue contribution across different segments
- Time-based sales and order trends

---

## 📊 Analysis Areas

### 1. Business Overview
Analysis of customers, sellers, products, categories, orders, and the overall marketplace structure.

### 2. Sales & Revenue Analysis
Analysis of revenue, average order value, category performance, customer spending, seller revenue, and state-level revenue.

### 3. Order & Time Analysis
Analysis of order trends, monthly activity, day-of-week patterns, and delivery timelines.

### 4. Payment Analysis
Analysis of payment methods, installments, payment revenue, and average order value.

### 5. Customer Analysis
Analysis of customer spending, order frequency, purchasing behavior, and customer-level KPIs.

### 6. Seller Analysis
Analysis of seller revenue, order volume, product coverage, and seller performance.

### 7. Review & Customer Satisfaction Analysis
Analysis of review scores across products, sellers, categories, and customer states.

### 8. Freight & Delivery Analysis
Analysis of freight costs, delivery duration, delayed deliveries, and logistics performance.

### 9. Revenue Contribution Analysis
Analysis of revenue contribution across geographic and customer segments.

### 10. Advanced SQL & Window Functions
Advanced analytical queries using ranking, window functions, CTEs, and time-based comparisons.
